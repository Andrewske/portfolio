### 10. Outro

**Location:** After "before you've written a single line of code."

**Suggestion:** "Hammond's original plan would have worked perfectly, assuming nothing went wrong. This workflow exists because things go wrong, and the best time to find that out is before you've written a single line of code.

Besides — Hammond's plan *did* work. He opened the park. The problems started when he assumed the plan was finished."

**Why it works:** Dark twist on Hammond's arc. The park opened. The plan wasn't the problem. The assumption that planning ends at implementation was.

**Meme potential:** Hammond's "We've spared no expense" as "We've skipped no phases" — the workflow version.
